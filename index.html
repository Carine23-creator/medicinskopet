Enzyme converting testosterone to estrogen. Shapes hormone balance and mood.

Key genetics

HLA-DQB1*06:02 is present in >90% of Type 1 narcolepsy cases, yet only ~25% of the general population carry it. Understanding your genoset can be a powerful step in the diagnostic journey.
